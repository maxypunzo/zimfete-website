How ZimFete works

Your subscription becomes someone’s loan — and one day, it comes back around

ZimFete is on record fundraising through monthly subscriptions and personal account top-ups, so members always have funding options for personal and family projects. Every dollar saved stays inside the cooperative, circulating as loans, asset finance and agribusiness capital before returning to the fund with interest.

That cycle has driven genuine, measurable empowerment since 2024 — and it only gets stronger as more members join.
